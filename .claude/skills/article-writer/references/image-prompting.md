# Image Prompting Guide — Howard Wedding Rentals Blog

Read this before writing prompts for Stage 4. Every image must serve two goals at once:
visually compelling enough to stop a scroll, and specific enough to match the article's topic.

---

## Visual Identity Rules (include in every prompt)

Every prompt must reinforce the brand aesthetic. These are non-negotiable:

- **Style:** Photorealistic wedding photography — not illustration, not 3D render, not clip art
- **Lighting:** Warm, soft natural light or golden-hour glow. Avoid harsh flash or clinical white light
- **Depth:** Soft bokeh background, shallow depth of field — the subject is sharp, the background is dreamy
- **Materials:** Rustic elegance — solid hardwood, linen, greenery, candles, florals
- **Setting:** Southern/Kentucky aesthetic — outdoor farmland, barn venues, garden ceremonies, open sky
- **No faces:** Avoid posed couples or clearly identifiable people (avoids likeness issues and dates the image)
- **No text:** No signs, chalkboard text, printed names, or overlaid graphics visible in the scene
- **No watermarks, no logos**

---

## Cover Image Template

Wide establishing shot. Headline-worthy. Shows the full scene at a glance.

```
[specific scene describing the article's primary subject],
wedding photography style, wide shot, warm golden natural light,
soft bokeh background, romantic and elegant atmosphere,
rustic hardwood elements, Southern outdoor venue,
photorealistic, high resolution, no text, no people
```

**The cover should answer:** "What is this article about?" in one image.

---

## Body Image Template

Close-up detail. Tied to the most visually descriptive section of the article.
Placed after the first H2 section (~1/3 through the article).

```
[specific detail related to the section it follows],
close-up wedding detail photography, warm ambient lighting,
soft focus background, elegant rustic setting,
photorealistic, no text, no faces
```

**The body image should answer:** "What does this specific thing look like?"

## Topic-to-Visual Mapping

### Seating chart articles (boards, templates, ideas, how-to)

**Cover options:**
- Custom printed foam board seating chart on a tall wooden easel at a reception entrance,
  surrounded by greenery and candlelight, guests blurred in background
- Bird's-eye view of a beautifully set reception with round tables, white linens,
  florals, and place cards, warm golden light, elegant barn venue

**Body options:**
- Close-up of a printed seating chart foam board with elegant typography,
  floral accent in the corner, soft background blur
- Close-up of a hand-lettered table number card on a linen tablecloth,
  candle and small floral arrangement beside it

### Place card articles

**Cover options:**
- Reception table detail shot — tented place cards at each seat, white linen,
  candles, soft florals, warm candlelight, elegant and intimate

**Body options:**
- Close-up of a single tented name card on a linen napkin with a small floral sprig,
  shallow depth of field, warm light
- Flat-lay of multiple place card styles (tented, acrylic, luggage tag)
  on white linen, soft natural window light

### Church pew rental articles

**Cover options:**
- Rows of solid hardwood church pews set up in an outdoor ceremony on a farm,
  open blue sky, golden afternoon light, aisle down the center, no people
- Side view of wooden church pews with simple ribbon and greenery pew end decorations,
  outdoor garden setting, dappled light through trees

**Body options:**
- Close-up of a solid hardwood church pew end decorated with white ribbon and small
  greenery swag, warm natural light, soft background blur
- Wide shot of a ceremony aisle flanked by wooden pews with floral arrangements at
  the ends, outdoor setting, golden hour

## Anti-Pattern Reference

Never include these in prompts — they produce off-brand results:

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| "illustration" / "cartoon" / "clip art" | Wrong style entirely |
| "3D render" / "CGI" | Looks artificial |
| "couple posing" / "bride and groom" | Faces cause likeness issues and date the photo |
| "chalkboard with text" / "sign with writing" | Text in images looks broken or wrong |
| "bright white studio light" | Clinical, cold — opposite of the brand warmth |
| "modern minimalist" / "corporate" | Wrong aesthetic for rustic Southern weddings |
