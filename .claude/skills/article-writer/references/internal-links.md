## Topic → link mapping (the primary topics this brand writes about)
- **Wedding seating / guest assignments / reception layout** → Seating Chart Tool (the free tool is the hero link), then Shop (foam boards, place cards).
- **Wedding guest experience / comfort / ceremony seating** → Services (pew rentals), Seating Chart Tool, Shop (place cards help guests find their seat).
- **Ceremony décor / aisle / pew styling** → Services (pew rentals), About (who sets it up).
- **General wedding planning / decision fatigue** → Services, the relevant tool/product, and a Contact CTA.

---

## Linking rules
- Aim for **3–5 internal links** per 1,500–2,000 word post. Enough to guide, never stuffed.
- Anchor text should read like natural speech, not a keyword. Good: "design it with our free seating chart tool." Avoid: "click here" or exact-match keyword spam.
- **Every post ends with one soft, on-brand CTA** — usually to Contact (book / check your date) or to the most relevant product page. Keep it calm, never "the clock is ticking."
- 0–2 **external** links to genuinely authoritative, non-competitor sources are fine for credibility. Never link a competing rental or stationery business.
